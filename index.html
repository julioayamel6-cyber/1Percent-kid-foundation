01 — Our Mission

Every kid
deserves a shot.

The 1% Kid Foundation gives young athletes the mentorship, literacy tools, and competitive sports training that used to be reserved for the few. We believe the next generation of leaders is built at the intersection of mind and body — where reading builds vision and sport builds discipline.

Our camps combine inspirational mentorship from D1 athletes and entrepreneurs with hands-on football and soccer training, financial literacy workshops, and competitive gameplay. No cost. No barriers. Just opportunity.
